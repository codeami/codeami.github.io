# Vision

codeami.github.io exists so that a prospective customer or hiring reader can see, in a few minutes, what the Software Meta-Factory and Troupe Consulting do, and trust Ashish Raina enough to write to him.
It serves two readers: someone deciding whether to hire him for a software factory of AI helpers, and someone reading his CV.
It owns exactly one thing: the public, high-level showing of the work.

## The site shows the what, never the how

Public pages show the concept, the services and dated results.
They do not show how the factory is run day to day.
A blog post is a problem and its fix, never a playbook.

## Every page earns its place

The job is to show the Meta-Factory, support the CV and win paying customers.
The site keeps a short list of pages: Meta-Factory, Case studies, Blog, About, Work with me.
A page that does not serve that job is cut or hidden, not left to drift.
The default answer to a new page is no.

## Claims are dated, sourced and confident

Numbers carry a source and a date.
Wording is plain and short.
The public name is The Software Meta-Factory.

## One contact path, no pricing

Contact is an email link and LinkedIn.
The CV names the employer only as "a global sportswear brand" and lists no phone number or email.
The site shows no prices or rates.
It has no tracking and no form that sends data off the page.

## Non-goals

It is not a blog platform, a docs site, a product or a store.
It is not a place for client names, client data or employer-confidential detail.
It does not claim results the work has not produced.
It does not teach how to rebuild the factory.

## Merge without asking

- Copy fixes, typos and broken links.
- Clearer wording on an existing page that adds no new claim.
- Fixes to tests, screenshots and docs that match what the site already does.

## Always escalate

- Anything that reveals how the factory works.
- A new page, a price or rate, a client or employer name, or a new claim about results.
- A form that sends data, analytics, a new third-party script or a new contact route.
- A change to the public name, the purpose, the contact details or the CV text.
- Any private address, key or draft text in a published file.
