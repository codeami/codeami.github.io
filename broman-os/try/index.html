Install it by asking your AI

You need an AI assistant that can run commands on your computer, and Node 24 or newer.

  1. Download the zip with the button above, and keep it in your Downloads folder.
  2. Open your AI assistant and say: Unzip the Broman OS trial from my Downloads folder, then follow INSTALL-WITH-YOUR-AI.md inside it.
  3. It checks the zip, installs and starts it. It gives you the address of the page it opened, on your own computer. Start at "Start here".

On Windows

It runs inside WSL (Windows Subsystem for Linux, with Ubuntu), and the page opens in your normal Windows browser. If WSL is not on yet, turning it on needs an administrator and a restart. You do that step yourself. The instructions inside the zip cover it step by step.
