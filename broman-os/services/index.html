Three phases

01

Look

Days 1 to 3

We find the one stream of work that costs you the most attention.

What happens

  • A short call about how you work with Claude Code today.
  • We pick one stream of work: the approvals and open tasks you chase most.
  • We write down what a good result looks like.

What you get

  • One page naming the stream and the result you agreed.
  • A yes or a stop from you. Nothing else is started.
02

Prove

Days 4 to 14

One stream, on your real work, with you watching.

What happens

  • Your approvals and open tasks appear on one screen.
  • It first runs beside your normal way of working, so nothing depends on it yet.
  • You use it for real and tell us what is wrong.

What you get

  • A working screen on your own work.
  • A plain list of what held up and what did not.
  • The choice to keep it or stop.
03

Run

Month to month

It stays on your work, and you stay the one who approves.

What happens

  • It keeps running on the stream you proved.
  • A monthly look at what it did and what you changed.
  • New streams are added one at a time, each with its own gates.

What you get

  • A monthly note: what ran, what you approved, what you turned down.
  • The same stop at any month end.

Gates you control

Each gate has a day and a condition. If the condition is not met, you stop, and nothing is owed.

  1. Day 3

    Fit gate

    Passes when you can name the one stream of work, and we both agree what a good result looks like. If not, we stop here.

  2. Day 10

    First screen gate

    Passes when you see your own approvals and open tasks on one screen and can act on them. If not, we say so and you choose: fix or stop.

  3. Day 14

    Keep-or-stop gate

    You decide. Keep it and move to Run, or stop and keep everything you have.
