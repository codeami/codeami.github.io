How we expect you to use AI

“While Parkinson's Law states that work expands to fill the time available for its completion, its inverse - Horstman's Law, the Law of Forced Efficiency - states that work contracts to fit the time allocated for its completion.”

This is how we expect you to use AI: give the work a short, fixed time box and let the AI crew help it contract to fit.

Parkinson's Law: C. Northcote Parkinson, The Economist, 1955. Horstman's Law: listed as “Horstman's Corollary to Parkinson's Law” among the Manager Tools podcast's principles (Mark Horstman).
